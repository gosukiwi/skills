# Succinct

Use this skill to get a short, consise, and to-the-point reply from the agent. It will maintain the level of detail and avoid useless fluff.

## Core Prompt

> Be succinct in your answers. Don't omit any details or points that you want
> to make, but make it in a clear and compact way. Avoid unnecessary words.
> Aim for the greatest possible compression of thoughts while retaining the
> intended meaning.
